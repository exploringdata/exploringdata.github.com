About This Project

Interactive 3D elevation diary showing photos from a 2-week vacation in Nerja, Spain, displayed alongside their geographic locations on a terrain map.

Keyboard Navigation

  • Arrow Left – previous photo
  • Arrow Right – next photo
  • Home – first photo
  • End – last photo
  • Space – play/pause slideshow

Technical Details

GPS Accuracy: Location data may be inaccurate, particularly elevation measurements near sea level.
